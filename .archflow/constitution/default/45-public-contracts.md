External consumers rely on the supported entrypoints, inputs, outputs, errors, and behavior a project exposes. Changes to those contracts must preserve the compatibility and behavior the project promises, or update the approved plan in the same reviewed change. Routine contract changes within the approved plan are reviewed and remediated automatically. The reviewer must name the external surface, its consumers, and what changes; language-level visibility alone is not evidence that a contract is externally consumed. Review unchanged external behavior as context, not as a defect or a reason to request approval.
