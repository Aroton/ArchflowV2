Implementation follows a reviewed plan: it starts after the plan has been reviewed and its material findings resolved, and a run's plan after a human approved it. The plan stays truthful as work proceeds: a result that departs from the approved plan's requirements, architecture, or task scope updates the plan in the same reviewed change, where the project's approval rules and the plan-change rule decide whether a human must approve the change. A departure from an approved plan is reported as a drift finding against the plan, not as a failure of this rule. Explicit human-review triggers are honored.
