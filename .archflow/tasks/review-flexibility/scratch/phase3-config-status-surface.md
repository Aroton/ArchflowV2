But `planStateTransition` (transitions.ts) makes phase crossings *require* authenticated approvals (`hasAuthenticatedArtifactApproval`/combined design approval at 463-474; `committedOutput` requiring authenticated commit-authorization + `commit_observed` for phase-impl at 395-459) — a rule-advanced phase needs an alternative exit authority there.
