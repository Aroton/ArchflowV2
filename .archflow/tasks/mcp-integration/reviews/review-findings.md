## Resolved owner decisions

The five `ASK` findings were resolved by the owner and integrated into the architecture:

1. **B-1:** no Phase 0; keep premise validation in the implementation plan.
2. **B-2:** trust the supported first-party CLI's own subscription-auth access; ArchFlow still denies repository/global context and unrelated secrets and never forwards or logs credentials.
3. **M-1:** pin all routing configuration immutably per task; edits fail closed and require a new/upgraded task.
4. **M-8:** split the current persistence phase into three independently testable phases and keep proof ownership in the implementing phase.
5. **M-9:** reuse tracked Git blobs; apply 25 MiB/result and 250 MiB/task copied-payload caps with explicit failure and recorded pruning rules.

## Design lineage follow-up

The owner confirmed that [docs/mcp-integration-design.md](../../../../docs/mcp-integration-design.md) is the originating design. The architecture now cites it explicitly as design lineage while stating that the approved PRD and architecture are normative where the documents differ; the source design remains preserved rather than treated as unrelated obsolete material.
