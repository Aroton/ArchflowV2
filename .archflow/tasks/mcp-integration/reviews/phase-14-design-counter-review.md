# Phase 14 Design Counter-Review

- **blocker — The adjudicator is allowed to mint the “current” mechanical evidence that its own pass depends on.** The design explicitly leaves `enforced_by` states adjudicator-reported and checks only label coverage and digest shape (phase design lines 18, 35, and 102). Live `validateAdjudicationClaims` rejects a `current` entry only when its `subject_digest` is wrong; it does not authenticate `state` or `evidence_digest` against a server observation (`src/contracts/adjudication.ts:112-123`). A child can therefore claim `task-path-boundary-tests` or `subject-digest-validation` is current with an invented digest and produce `constitution: "pass"`, missing REQ-15, REQ-17, REQ-33, and VAL-15. The `adjudicator-judgement` fallback compounds the problem by presenting the pinned policy digest as mechanical evidence of artifact compliance merely to satisfy the frozen non-empty-pass check. **Suggested resolution:** derive each declared mechanism's state and evidence digest from server-observed facts and require the model result to match them exactly; the model may explain those facts but must not author them. For rules with no declared `enforced_by`, amend the Phase 2 semantic check to allow `pass` with an empty evidence list instead of synthesizing a fake mechanism. Keep the implementation direct for the two labels currently shipped rather than introducing a predicate framework.

- **blocker — Self-review and triage are neither durable nor backed by the authority references used to advance.** The design defers both ingestion routes to Phase 15, passes them into `assessCurrentEvidence` as caller-supplied branded values, and fabricates self-review authority using a fixed result ID, `result_digest = evidence_digest`, and the current revision (lines 28, 32, 53, and 104). REQ-08 and REQ-11 require every durable step artifact—and specifically self-review input/result digests—to be bound by `archflow_state`; REQ-13, REQ-21, and REQ-22 require restartable current-evidence decisions. The frozen `result_digest` domain is the canonical `ResultIdentityPayload`, not the evidence payload (`phase-2-review-evidence-and-contract-authority.md:281-295`), while live qualification does not cross-check agent-declared result/revision fields (`src/contracts/trust.ts:181-202,232-241`). After restart, durable state therefore cannot reconstruct the exact self/counter set that authorized triage or prove that the supplied triage was its once-only disposition. **Suggested resolution:** choose the self-review/triage ingestion and retention contract in this phase, commit both through authenticated transactions, and make assessment reload all required evidence plus its authentic result/receipt authority from durable state. Derive the self-review authority references from the correlated state result; do not synthesize them. Phase 15 should wire the chosen contract rather than design it.

- **blocker — The proposed generic production minters erase the trust boundary the brands were created to enforce.** Line 27 deliberately makes minting discipline a review convention and proposes production counterparts to the raw test factories in `src/contracts/internal/test-capabilities.ts:25-38`. Live referenced-evidence parsing accepts `evidence_digest` independently from the payload (`src/contracts/review.ts:181-184`, `src/contracts/adjudication.ts:153-156`), and `authorityQualifier` only compares the repeated link/wrapper digest plus selected provenance fields (`src/contracts/trust.ts:181-202`). A clone-and-brand `createVerifiedEvidence` or `createEvidenceAuthorityLink` can thus authenticate mismatched payloads or invented result/receipt/revision facts; keeping it out of the public barrel does not protect production callers. This contradicts the frozen rule that only the canonical-content and persisted-authority owners mint these brands. **Suggested resolution:** expose narrow owner-specific constructors: one computes `canonicalJsonDigest` internally from parsed evidence, one reloads and verifies a retained manifest, and authority-link constructors derive result/receipt/revision fields from authenticated transaction or checkpoint documents. Do not add generic raw branding functions for production.

- **blocker — Same-family adjudication cannot be represented as the degraded result the design promises.** The design permits same-family adjudication and says it is labelled degraded (lines 42 and 117), but `observationSource.observeAdjudication` always returns `ServerAttestedAdjudication` (`src/contracts/trust.ts:107-118`). `DegradedAdjudication` is a different assurance arm, and its authority requires `DegradedAuthorityReferences {checkpoint_digest, checkpoint_revision}` (`src/contracts/adjudication.ts:141-153`, `src/contracts/trust.ts:122-151`). A normal same-family CLI dispatch has no manual checkpoint, so the proposed minters must either leave it server-attested with no degraded label or fabricate checkpoint provenance. **Suggested resolution:** for this prototype, reject automatic same-family adjudication and leave degraded evidence to the existing checkpoint-backed manual path. If same-family automatic fallback is required, amend the frozen evidence and authority contracts with an explicit observed-dispatch degraded provenance arm before implementing it.

- **blocker — The sole new rewind cannot enact the retry and redirect decisions opened after adjudication.** The design adds only `triage/succeeded -> produce/running` (lines 29 and 92), but `runAdjudication` is specified to retain `adjudicate/succeeded` and then open `review-trigger`, `material-drift`, or `adjudication-failure` (line 102). Phase 12 maps `revise` and `revise-current` to retry, while gate closure removes `open_gate` without changing step/status (`src/contracts/gates.ts:105-116`, `src/state/gates.ts:497-510`). Live `legalMovement` permits neither a succeeded same-step retry nor `adjudicate -> produce` (`src/state/transitions.ts:81-98`). `amend-upstream` also changes the fingerprint while leaving the state at the old adjudication boundary. **Suggested resolution:** pin the legal, gate-authorized transition for every retry/redirect outcome from `adjudicate/succeeded`, including how a changed upstream fingerprint re-enters production, and cover gate-decision replay plus crashes between gate closure and re-entry.

- **blocker — `retry-once` cannot durably authorize an attempt beyond `max_attempts`.** The design says exhaustion always opens `attempts-exhausted` and that the gate decision is the outcome (lines 36-37 and 104), but non-advancing retry decisions add no approval, receipt, or other state marker; `nextStateForRecord` merely removes the gate and preserves the exhausted attempt (`src/state/gates.ts:497-517`). On the next assessment—especially after restart—the same state still satisfies `attempt >= max_attempts`, so it must reopen the same gate rather than consume the promised retry. **Suggested resolution:** define a durable, replayable one-shot retry authorization that is consumed by exactly one next attempt, or have resolution of `retry-once` atomically perform the authorized transition and retain a state/receipt binding to that decision. Add a crash test after decision archival/gate closure and before the retried work begins.

- **blocker — Dropping the tool-name check from result installation loses request, task, and repository authority.** The replacement guard checks target/reference/embedded artifact shape but declares tool-name removal safe solely because the WeakMap capability is one-shot (lines 31 and 51). The capability authenticates installation facts, not which request may consume them (`src/state/transaction.ts:113-150`). Standalone manifest semantics correlate source to manifest but not manifest to the current state (`src/contracts/durable.ts:533-603`); evidence tool calls carry no request artifact, so they also lose the task check that live `artifactMatches` provides for produce (`src/state/transitions.ts:102-120`). An evidence installation can consequently be attached to an artifact-less `archflow_state` call (creating a `record-state-boundary` receipt that the proposed resume list will not restore), or a valid same-phase/fingerprint manifest prepared for another task can pass the stated predicate. The verification contract also demands classified failures although live `buildPlan` deliberately throws for these trusted-callback invariant violations (`src/state/transaction.ts:520-550,919-959`). **Suggested resolution:** retain an exact tool-to-step-to-source-kind mapping and additionally bind manifest task, repository identity, authenticated task root/targets, reference, and target state before consumption. Decide explicitly whether violations are programmer-invariant throws or `ProjectResult` failures and make the tests/API promise match; do not claim both.

- **major — Multiple simultaneous material upstream drifts do not fit the frozen gate interface.** Adjudication requires one `drift_finding` for every approved upstream and permits several material findings (`src/contracts/adjudication.ts:112-127`), while `material-drift` carries exactly one `affected_upstream` and one claim list (`src/contracts/gates.ts:34,88`). Line 102 only maps aggregate `drift: "material"` to that gate, leaving selection, ordering, and the fate of additional material upstreams unspecified. An implementation handling design and phase-design drift together must invent replay semantics or later widen the Phase 12 contract. **Suggested resolution:** deterministically serialize one material upstream per gate and require re-adjudication after each resolution so the next still-material upstream gates, or amend the gate context now to carry the complete sorted material set. Test at least two material upstreams with different decisions.

- **major — Deferring gate-counter digest reconciliation leaves two incompatible meanings of `evidence_digest` across one review authority seam.** Phase 14 pins canonical evidence-JSON identity but assigns gate-counter reconciliation to Phase 15 (lines 25-26 and 52). The live Phase 12 ledger authenticates a supplemental review by comparing the rendered Markdown file digest to its slot's `evidence_digest` (`src/state/gates.ts:304-328`), and the wait path can return a supplied digest without checking file completeness (`src/state/gate-wait.ts:72-90`). Because `renderReviewEvidence` embeds the evidence digest in its own bytes (`src/contracts/renderers.ts:34-43`), the file digest cannot also be canonical payload identity. Leaving this open will force Phase 15 to change the generic minter or retrofit the gate watcher/ledger after Phase 14 has built against the new rule. **Suggested resolution:** pin the interface now: file appearance and completeness are only wake-up signals; supplemental authority comes from parsed/validated JSON or a retained manifest whose artifact digest is the canonical evidence digest. Update the wait and ledger contracts together, and never let gate-counter reviews use a second digest meaning.

## Triage

Triaged 2026-07-30. Eight findings accepted in full, one accepted in part. Nothing rejected outright. Load-bearing claims were re-verified against live code before disposition.

| Finding | Disposition | What changed |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Adjudicator mints its own `current` mechanical evidence | **Accepted in part** | Rejected the suggestion that the server derive each mechanism's state from server-observed facts: the two shipped labels are test suites, and executing them from the adjudication path is a subsystem no requirement asks for and the envelope does not pay for. Accepted the substance in two cheaper pins that *remove* machinery. The fabricated `adjudicator-judgement` entry is **deleted** along with its label constant, pinned digest, negative control, and human-judgement question; the frozen rule is amended so `pass` is legal with an **empty** `enforced_by`, with "empty iff the rule declares none" enforced by the registry-aware cross-check. Separately, a rule that *does* declare `enforced_by` cannot reach `pass` on a model-asserted `current` — the envelope instructs `uncertain`/`unknown`, which the frozen fold gates. Exactly one of the four shipped rules is affected; the scoped-waiver path is the designed human escape. |
| Self-review and triage neither durable nor authority-backed | **Accepted** | Transport resolved in this phase instead of deferred. `archflow_state.artifact` goes from five members to seven. This deleted the synthesized self-review authority carve-out, the caller-supplied parameters on `assessCurrentEvidence`, and the deferral amendment. Confirmed the reviewer's catch that the carve-out put a wrong-domain value in a frozen `result_digest` field. |
| Generic production minters erase the trust boundary | **Accepted** | No production counterparts to the raw brand factories. Replaced with owner-specific constructors that derive rather than accept; the "minting discipline" review-obligation framing is deleted and the constraint is now structural. |
| Same-family adjudication cannot be represented as degraded | **Accepted** | Automatic same-family adjudication is refused as `FAMILY_MISMATCH` before launch. Degraded adjudication remains only on the checkpoint-backed manual path. Recorded as a deliberate **narrowing** of the architecture's stated position. |
| The sole rewind cannot enact post-adjudication retry/redirect | **Accepted** | The branch is generalized to re-entry to `produce`/`running` from `succeeded` at either `triage` or `adjudicate`. A follow-up round found the adjudicate-side authorizing precondition is still not computable from durable state; that is being fixed. |
| `retry-once` cannot durably authorize an attempt past the bound | **Accepted** | Verified the livelock: `nextStateForRecord` appends an `ApprovalRef` only on an `advance` effect, so a retry decision writes nothing and the gate reopens forever. Counting approvals cannot fix it. Gate closure now enacts the authorized transition for retry-effect `attempts-exhausted` decisions, recorded as a deliberate Phase 12 guard-site extension. |
| Dropping the tool-name check loses request/task/repository authority | **Accepted** | This corrected an error in the orchestrator's own earlier triage. The exact tool → step → source-kind mapping is retained and extended with manifest task identity, repository identity, and authenticated-target binds. The throws-versus-classified-failures question is decided once, per violation category, with tests matching. |
| Multiple simultaneous material drifts do not fit the gate interface | **Accepted** | Serialized deterministically — one material upstream per gate in `approved_upstream_digests` order, with re-adjudication between — rather than widening the frozen Phase 12 context. |
| Deferring gate-counter digest reconciliation | **Accepted** | Changed from a deferral to a decision. File appearance and completeness are wake-up signals only; supplemental authority comes from parsed JSON or a retained manifest. The wait and ledger contracts move together as one guard site. |

Net effect: four of the nine fixes delete machinery. Guard sites grew from five to eight. Two prior human-judgement questions were answered by these decisions and removed; two new ones were added.

## Round 2

- **blocker — The evidence-wrapper amendment leaves live durable invariants on the old top-level artifact shape.** The design pins a special result-manifest correlation arm at `src/contracts/durable.ts:533-554`, but the two new `DurableArtifact` members also enter the ordinary artifact slot. Live `artifactInvalid` and state/artifact agreement read `artifact.task_id` at `durable.ts:269-272` and `:832-833`, while the proposed wrappers put that field under `.evidence`; `isStepped` admits only document and implementation artifacts at `:312-316`, so the existing phase/step/fingerprint agreement at `:871-880` would not cover evidence artifacts. The manifest arm has two further incompatible assumptions: it first validates every `source_artifact` through the five-member `DurableArtifact` slot at `:533-536` even though `adjudication-evidence` deliberately does not join that union, and it compares `snapshot_digest` to `source.snapshot_digest` at `:552-553` although none of the three thin evidence wrappers has that field. Implementing only the pinned payload-digest deviation therefore either fails typecheck or leaves completed durable-state correlations weaker for evidence. **Suggested resolution:** pin one evidence-source accessor/validation branch used consistently by both artifact-slot and result-manifest semantics: derive task/phase/step/fingerprint from `.evidence`, validate adjudication evidence directly without adding it to the MCP artifact union, and define the evidence snapshot check from the manifest outputs/projections rather than a nonexistent source field. Add state-plus-artifact negative tests as well as manifest tests.

- **blocker — Gate resolution cannot compute the newly required produce-entry fingerprint from the inputs it receives.** The design says retry-effect closure calls the existing `resolve_input_fingerprint`, but that resolver requires a complete `FingerprintReadContext` containing a parsed tool call and live config (`src/state/fingerprint.ts:27-29`; `src/state/read.ts:31-43`), and call semantics select the phase and rubric portions of the subject (`src/state/fingerprint.ts:40-60,97-106`). `GateOpenInput` has no parsed `archflow_state` produce-entry call or config snapshot (`src/state/gates.ts:56-72`), the archived gate request does not preserve them, and `resolveDurableGate` receives only authority, gate ID, an optional asserted current fingerprint, and supplemental outcome. `validateLiveGateState` merely compares that assertion to the current state's old fingerprint (`gates.ts:241-254`); it cannot derive the new re-entry value. **Suggested resolution:** pin a narrow server-owned produce-entry fingerprint API whose inputs gate resolution actually has (and add any missing canonical inputs explicitly), or carry an authenticated parsed call plus live config through resolution. The enacted state must use that derived value, never the caller assertion or the pre-gate fingerprint.

- **blocker — Retry-effect gate closure bypasses the transition kernel without an equivalent legal-predecessor guard.** The normal re-entry amendment carefully limits movement to succeeded `triage|adjudicate` → `produce/running`, in the same phase instance, at `attempt + 1`. The proposed `nextStateForRecord` path instead becomes a second step/status writer. Live gate opening does not require `input.phase_instance === current.phase_instance` or bind each gate context to the current step/status/attempt (`src/state/gates.ts:331-490`), and live `nextStateForRecord` constructs state directly without `planStateTransition` (`:497-510`). As pinned, a syntactically valid retry-effect archive can therefore enact from a running, wrong-step, wrong-phase, or mismatched-attempt predecessor that `legalMovement` would reject; the listed tests cover only successful landing states. **Suggested resolution:** introduce one shared gate-authorized re-entry planner that authenticates and removes the exact open gate, verifies request/current phase and decision-specific context (including `attempts-exhausted.context.step`, `attempts`, and `maximum_attempts`), and then reuses the transition draft rules. Add negative tests for wrong step, status, phase instance, and attempt before allowing archive enactment.

**Escalated rather than revised.** These are the sixth review round on this design and the third consecutive round to find blockers in the previous round's fixes. All three land in the frozen-contract prologue (chunks 1-2, nine guard sites) rather than in Phase 14's own orchestration, and each suggested resolution adds further machinery to Phases 7, 9, and 12. That prologue is repair work for gaps in completed phases, not Phase 14 scope, so the structural question is being put to the human before another revision pass rather than absorbed here.
